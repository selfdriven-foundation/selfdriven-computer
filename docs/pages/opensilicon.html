Architecture / Open Silicon

Trust needs a verifiable floor.

Every layer of selfdriven.computer runs on a chip: the keys that sign key events, the processor that sequences them, the runtime that acts. Open Silicon — chips built with free tools, open IP and published manufacturing know-how that anyone can inspect and build on — makes that floor something you can check rather than assume.

Why it matters here

Verification has to reach the silicon.

The six-layer model makes every agent action sequenced, signed and witnessed. Each of those guarantees finally executes on a chip — and a closed chip is the one component that still asks for blind trust.

  1. 01 · Keys

    Hardware holds the root

    KERI signing and pre-rotation keys are only as safe as the silicon that stores and uses them. An open root of trust can be inspected end to end.

  2. 02 · Sequence

    The clock runs on a chip

    Layer 01's cryptographic sequence is computed by a processor. Open designs show what that hardware is meant to do — and open fabrication lets you check that it does.

  3. 03 · Runtime

    Execution you can audit

    Layer 04 runtimes are stateless and disposable by design. On open silicon, the hardware beneath them can be audited too, not just the code.

  4. 04 · Attestation

    Identity from the device up

    Open roots of trust such as OpenTitan and Caliptra provide device identity and measured boot — evidence that fits the same verify-don't-trust model as the key event log.

Design

Open PDKs and tools.

Until 2019, every commercially relevant process design kit sat behind a licence and an NDA. Three foundries now publish theirs, and open flows take a design from RTL to manufacturable layout.

  • github.com/googleSkyWater SKY130130nm — the first open process design kit, now stewarded under the CHIPS Alliance.
  • github.com/googleGlobalFoundries GF180MCU180nm, aimed at microcontrollers and higher-voltage mixed-signal designs.
  • github.com/IHP-GmbHIHP SG13G2130nm BiCMOS from Germany's IHP research foundry, for analog, digital, mixed-signal and RF design.
  • fossi-foundation.orgLibreLaneSuccessor to OpenLane, which delivered 800+ tapeouts. One config file takes a design to GDS on all three open PDKs.
  • openroad.orgOpenROADOpen place-and-route, used for commercial tapeouts from 180nm down to 12nm.
  • github.com/RTimothyEdwardsOpen PDKsBuilds SkyWater and GlobalFoundries foundry data into the form open EDA tools need.
Fabricate

Shuttles that make it affordable.

When Efabless shut down in early 2025, the community lost its main path to low-volume silicon. New shuttle services have since filled the gap.

  • wafer.spacewafer.spaceSingapore-based wafer-sharing service on GF180MCU. A 1,000-die shuttle slot starts at $3,000.
  • chipfoundry.ioChipFoundryRestarted SkyWater 130nm multi-project wafer shuttles after Efabless closed.
